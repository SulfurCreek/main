## 原文

### The Truth About Résumés in 2026: Navigating AI and Applicant Tracking Systems

For years, the job search process has been dominated by a singular boogeyman: the Applicant Tracking System (ATS). Job seekers are told that "robots" are reading their résumés, assigning them match scores, and automatically rejecting them before human eyes ever see their applications. However, a deep dive into data from productivity experts, ex-Google recruiters, and major platform developers reveals a different reality. Résumés are still vital, but the strategies used to write them have been fundamentally misunderstood.

#### Mythbusting the "ATS Rejection Robot"
The most pervasive myth in modern hiring is that an ATS uses Artificial Intelligence to automatically filter out or reject candidates who lack specific keywords. Farah Sharghi, an ex-Google recruiter who has screened over 100,000 résumés, emphatically debunks this. Most ATS platforms are legacy databases—glorified spreadsheets used to store files, track interview progress, and allow recruiters to manually search for candidates.

They are not powered by advanced AI rejection algorithms. In fact, under federal rules like the OFCCP and EEOC in the United States, utilizing automated tools to blindly make hiring decisions without human review is prohibited to prevent bias and compliance issues. The silence candidates hear after applying is not the result of an AI rejecting them; it is the result of a human recruiter having too many résumés to review and simply overlooking theirs. The problem is not automated rejection; it is human invisibility.

#### Optimizing for the System: Format over Hacks
While the ATS isn't rejecting candidates, it is parsing their data, and formatting matters immensely. A survey of over 2,000 HR executives conducted by Harvard Business School and Accenture found that over 90% of employers use ATS platforms to initially filter candidates. If the software cannot read the file, the recruiter cannot find the candidate.

Productivity expert Jeff Su cites that 87% of hiring managers say their AI/ATS software reads simple text-based résumés more accurately than highly designed, visual ones. This means avoiding Canva templates, multiple columns, graphics, or "skill bars" (which both Teal and Su note are universally disliked by recruiters).

Instead, candidates must:
1. **Stick to standard formatting:** Use a single-column layout, standard fonts, and conventional headers (Summary, Experience, Education).
2. **Use readable files:** Both Jobscan and Su recommend standard PDFs (under 2.5 megabytes) or Word Documents.
3. **Avoid the "White Font" trick:** Hiding keywords in white text is a disastrous strategy. When an ATS parses a résumé, it converts everything to plain, uniform text. Recruiters will instantly see the hidden text, flagging the candidate as untrustworthy.

#### Tailoring and the "Keyword Mapping" Strategy
While keyword stuffing (cramming every phrase from a job description into a résumé regardless of actual experience) is harmful, strategic tailoring is essential. Su references a massive data study of nearly 2 million applications: untailored résumés saw a 3.09% interview rate, while tailored résumés achieved a 5.71% rate—an 84% increase. However, résumés with the *absolute highest* keyword coverage actually saw a 21% decrease in interviews.

Instead of stuffing, Teal and Jobscan recommend **Keyword Mapping**. This involves checking the job description's "Preferred Qualifications" (the employer's true wish list) and ensuring those specific terms appear naturally in the résumé. For example, if a job description emphasizes "SEO," that exact term should be present in both the skills section and the work history.

#### Writing for the Human: The 7-Second Rule
Ultimately, a résumé must pass the human test. According to Teal (who has hired thousands of candidates), recruiters read résumés in an "F pattern." They spend under two seconds skimming titles, roughly ten seconds scanning for keywords, and if hooked, they might spend a minute actually reading.

To survive this brutal triage, candidates must:
* **Quantify Impact:** Research shows résumés that quantify impact see 75% higher interview rates than those that merely list responsibilities.
* **Use the XYZ Formula:** Instead of writing "Responsible for social media," candidates should write: *"Increased engagement by 150% [Result + Metric] through strategic content calendar implementation [Context]."*
* **Address the Title Gap:** If applying for a role with a different title than your current one, Teal recommends adding context in parentheses (e.g., *Product Manager (Functionally leading roadmap)*) so the recruiter doesn't assume a lack of qualification during their 2-second skim.

#### The AI Era: Proving, Not Just Listing
Finally, as AI becomes standard in the workplace, simply listing "ChatGPT" as a skill is insufficient. An Oxford University experiment found that adding relevant AI skills increased a candidate's chance of an interview by up to 15 percentage points. However, 60% of hiring managers want candidates to prove these skills. Candidates should highlight exactly *how* they used AI to save time or increase output (e.g., *"Cut reporting time by 50% using Claude Code"*), linking to GitHub repositories or portfolios for concrete proof.

## 研讀筆記（session 整理）

### 核心觀念轉換
1. **ATS 不是淘汰機器人，是資料庫**：問題是「人找不到你」（human invisibility），不是「機器刷掉你」。→ 驗證重點從「騙過演算法」改成「**讓招募者搜得到、2 秒看得懂**」。104 也一樣：人資用關鍵字搜人才庫，所以職稱、摘要、技能欄的詞要是招募者會打的詞。
2. **格式比技巧重要**：單欄、標準字型、標準標題（摘要／工作經歷／學歷）、無圖表與技能進度條、PDF < 2.5MB 或 Word。
3. **禁止作弊**：白字藏關鍵字一律禁止（解析後變純文字，人一看就知道）。
4. **客製 +84%，但關鍵字覆蓋「最高」反而 −21%**：要做**關鍵字對應**（Keyword Mapping），不是塞滿。關鍵字覆蓋率的目標是**落在合理區間**，不是 100%。
5. **對應「加分條件（Preferred）」**：JD 的加分條件才是雇主真正的願望清單；那些詞要**同時出現在技能欄和工作經歷**，自然出現。
6. **7 秒規則／F 型閱讀**：標題 < 2 秒、關鍵字掃描約 10 秒、被勾住才讀 1 分鐘。→ 每條條列的**開頭**就要有結果或關鍵詞。
7. **量化影響 +75%**；XYZ 公式（結果＋指標＋做法）。
8. **職稱落差**：現職職稱和目標職稱不同時，用括號補脈絡（例：「產品企劃主任」→「Product Owner／PM（職稱：產品企劃主任）」，已採用）。
9. **AI 要證明，不是列名**：寫出**怎麼用 AI 省了多少時間或多產出什麼**，並附作品集／GitHub 連結當證據。

### 對步驟 3（寫 essay）的洞見
- 每篇 essay 都要能拆出 **X（結果）＋Y（指標）＋Z（做法）**；缺 Y 就標「指標待補」，不要讓 essay 只剩過程。
- 對照 JD 時，先看**加分條件**，再看必備條件；essay 用 JD 的原詞描述同一件事（例：JD 寫 UAT，就寫 UAT），但**不為 JD 新增事實**。
- 有用到 AI 的專案，essay 要寫**AI 做了哪一段、省了什麼、產出什麼**（例：搜尋邏輯衝突，借助 AI 把業務邏輯轉成工程條件），不只寫「使用 AI 工具」。數字沒有就不寫數字。
- essay 第一句寫結果或處境的關鍵，方便步驟 4 產出「開頭就有結果」的版本。
- 職稱落差、轉職動機這類「2 秒內會被誤判」的資訊，在 essay 就先寫清楚。
