【工作內容 / Job Description】
【About Yahoo EC】

Yahoo EC is focused on making the world’s daily habits more inspiring and entertaining. By creating highly personalized experiences for our users, we keep people connected to what matters most to them, across devices and around the globe. In turn, we create value for advertisers by connecting them with the audiences that build their businesses.

---------------------------
【About You 】
We are looking for a proactive and hands-on Product Manager to drive our customer engagement automation, leverage cutting-edge AI agent technologies, and empower internal business units. In this role, you will own enterprise messaging platforms, build light AI automation tools, transform data into executive insights, and act as an internal solution consultant to promote digital transformation across group companies.

You will need to possess a strong business sense, product development experience, strong leadership and communication skills in order to successfully champion the cause of consumers and Yahoo EC.    

This position requires an individual who can work closely with and influence cross-functional teams such as sales, product, marketing, engineering, and user experience design as well as analysts. This position may also require some interactions with counterparts located overseas through conference calls.

The ideal candidate would have a strong understanding of complex data while also having a solid understanding of Internet business, and the competitive landscape. Also experience interfacing with mid/high-level clients is a must. 

---------------------------
【Your Day / Key Responsibilities】

• MarTech System Ownership & Operations: Drive the end-to-end product strategy and execution for our customer messaging platform (Adobe Campaign Standard), managing eDM and Push Notification systems to fuel user growth and engagement.

• AI Agentic Tools & Automation: Identify workflow bottlenecks and build/deploy light AI agentic tools and scripts to automate internal processes and boost organizational efficiency.

• Internal Solution Consulting & Evangelism: Act as an internal solution consultant for group business units. Pitch, demonstrate, and facilitate the adoption of modern MarTech and AI solutions tailored to diverse stakeholder needs.

• Product Vision & Roadmap Management: Define product strategies, build clear roadmaps, prioritize competing opportunities, and set meaningful success metrics (KPIs) aligned with business priorities.

• Data Querying & Storytelling: Independently perform SQL/data queries to analyze solution performance. Build compelling "data stories" and visualizations for executives and key stakeholders to drive decisions.

• Cross-Functional Leadership: Collaborate closely with engineers, analysts, business leads, and external vendors to ensure high-quality, on-time delivery while building strong cross-team trust.

---------------------------
【Minimum Qualifications】
• BA/BS or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Business Analytics, Statistics, Information Management, or related technical disciplines.

• 8+ years of experience in Product/Project Management, Business Intelligence, Data Analytics, or Process Automation.
Practical experience operating enterprise MarTech/CRM tools (experience with Adobe Campaign Standard, Salesforce Marketing Cloud, or similar tools is preferred).

• Strong analytical skills with proven capability in database querying (SQL) and data manipulation to derive actionable business insights independently.

• Demonstrated ability to leverage analytics, metrics, and benchmarking to drive product decisions and present written/visual summaries to stakeholders.
