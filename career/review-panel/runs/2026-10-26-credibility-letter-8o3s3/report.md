# Credibility 盲測：104 自我推薦信（沛思坦【北士科】Product Manager，letter-r2）

## 整體形象一句話
「近 10 年經驗、6 萬家付費企業、AI 功能也上過線，履歷值得打開來看。不過三點裡只有第二點看得到結果，第一點是我最想問的 AI 經驗，卻只有過程，看不到成效；結尾又退回『希望』，收得太軟。」

## 還像執行流水帳，或寫得太溫吞的段落

| 原句（前 30 字） | 缺哪個角度 | 讀者疑問 | 改寫方向（只調語序或點破目的，不新增事實） |
|---|---|---|---|
| 破題：我有近 10 年產品經理經驗，現任人力銀行企業端招募系統… | 敢不敢講、破題 | 跟 JD 的加分條件（HR Tech、生成式 AI）有什麼關聯？ | 把對位詞拉到前 20 字：「我在 HR Tech 做了近 10 年產品，現任人力銀行企業端招募系統 Product Owner，負責約 6 萬家付費企業使用的系統改版，並主導生成式 AI 功能上線。」（「HR Tech」只是重新命名信中已有的人力銀行／招募系統，不算新事實） |

（以上沒有發現多餘的免責語；「840 件完成 743 件（88%）」的寫法乾淨，請保留。）

## 已經有 Senior 高度的段落
- **B2／A2「沒有文件＋必須沿用既有後台與資料庫 → 逆向還原 → 分階段上線 → 743/840」**：限制、怎麼判斷、結果三者都有，這是全信最有說服力的一點，不要動。
- **B1「制定勞動法規合規的驗收標準、AI 內容經企業確認才刊登」**：看得出他在管 AI 風險、做了取捨，不是單純把功能上線。這點直接對應 JD 的生成式 AI／LegalTech 加分條件，請保留。
- **B3「沒有直接競品可參考 → 訪談商家行銷單位 → 設計雙邊流程」**：從零開始做需求探索，正好對應 JD 的 user interviews，數字也具體。
- **破題「約 6 萬家付費企業」**：一句話就交代了規模和商業屬性。

## 英文自然度（方案 A）

| # | 原句 | 問題 | 建議改寫 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | To lower the barrier for employers posting jobs, I led the launch of AI job description generation and AI talent recommendations. | 「lower the barrier for employers posting jobs」直譯自中文；「AI job description generation」是名詞堆疊，讀起來硬 | **I led the launch of AI-generated job descriptions and AI talent recommendations to make job posting easier for employers.** |
| 1b | I set acceptance criteria that kept generated content compliant with labor law, required employers to confirm AI content before publishing, and tuned recommendation rules based on what clients actually selected. | 三個並列動詞的主體不一致（第二項要求的是企業）；「what clients actually selected」略口語 | **I defined acceptance criteria to keep AI output compliant with labor law, required employers to review AI content before it went live, and refined recommendation rules based on which candidates clients actually chose.**（「candidates」會讓主語更具體；如果實際選的不是人才，改回 "what clients selected"） |
| 2 | The recruitment system's account permissions and business rules had no documentation, yet the overhaul had to keep the existing back office and database. … Of 840 client requests …, 743 (88%) are now resolved. | 主句被背景拖住，結果在最後；「had no documentation」偏中式，較自然的說法是 "were undocumented"；「hands-on testing」語意模糊 | **Resolved 743 of 840 (88%) client requests on accounts, login, company data and contacts by rebuilding a recruitment system whose permission and business rules were undocumented, while keeping the existing back office and database. I reverse-engineered the rules by testing the live system and interviewing internal teams, then turned them into specs for phased releases.** |
| 3 | With no direct competitor to learn from, I interviewed merchants' marketing teams … then designed KOOL's two-sided matching flow. | 「merchants' marketing teams」不自然；KOOL 第一次出現卻沒有定義 | **With no direct competitor to benchmark against, I interviewed brands' marketing teams on how they sourced influencers, then designed the two-sided matching flow for KOOL, an influencer marketplace.** |
| 3b | The platform grew to about 16,000 influencer profiles and over 80,000 applications in six months. | 「applications」語意不清（可能被讀成求職申請） | **Within six months, the platform reached about 16,000 influencer profiles and more than 80,000 collaboration applications.** |

另外：A 版的破題與結尾都是中文，只有條列用英文。JD 要求能用中英文撰寫文件，這樣混用可以接受，但三點的時態要統一（目前 2 用 "are now resolved"，其餘是過去式；改寫後統一為過去式）。
