Dear Positive Gird Human Resource Managers,

The attached resume outlines a detailed look at my skills and experience. Also, I'd like to further elaborate why I think I'd be a great match for the position and a desirable fit for Positive Grid.

As a music lover and a guitar player, I've always been interested in the products of Positive Grid. It thrills me even more to know the company is partially based in Taiwan because the scope of Positive Grid is International level. Plus, coming from a product management/development background, I'm blown away by how the product line actually consists of both the software and hardware side. The team must've been extremely diligent and a blast to work with.

At my previous job as Project Manager at Sinful group, I proposed new product ideas and initiated them along with multiple teams of different sizes or even nationals. Several of the projects were in fact under limited resources and timeframes. While the company was primarily developing social platforms, my passion for building a good product which is able to grow remains the same: now for my fellow guitar community.

Please contact me if you'd like to schedule an appointment or any sorts of calls. I'd love to discuss what I might be able to bring to the table, and I'm also looking forward to know more about this position and its responsibilities.

Have a lovely day,
KunHaui
